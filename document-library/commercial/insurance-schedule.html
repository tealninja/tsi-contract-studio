Additional insured and waiver of subrogation

Buyer and its affiliates shall be named additional insureds on the CGL, automobile, and umbrella policies with respect to liability arising from Seller's performance, and Seller's insurance shall be primary and non-contributory to any insurance carried by Buyer. Each party waives, and shall cause its insurers to waive, all rights of subrogation against the other for loss covered by the policies required under this schedule, to the extent of that coverage.

Certificates and notice

Seller shall deliver certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverage to Buyer before commencing work at the Site and on each renewal, and shall provide 30 days' written notice of cancellation or material reduction, and 10 days' notice for non-payment of premium. A certificate does not amend, extend, or waive the coverage the policy actually provides. The required insurance sets minimum coverage and does not limit Seller's obligations or liability under the Agreement.
