This annex sets the surface-preparation and protective-coating standards for the Equipment supplied by Seller. Systems are selected for the humid, warm paper-mill environment; the wet-end and washdown zones receive the more resistant systems. Color and system selections shown for confirmation shall match the Buyer mill standard. [DRAFT NOTE: Confirm Buyer equipment color (RAL) and any corporate coating specification before release.]

1. Surface preparation

Ferrous, immersion / wet-endabrasive blast to SSPC-SP 10 / NACE No. 2 (near-white)
Ferrous, generalabrasive blast to SSPC-SP 6 / NACE No. 3 (commercial)
Machined / touch-up areasSSPC-SP 2 / SP 3 (hand or power tool) where blasting is impractical
Surface profile50–75 µm angular anchor pattern
Cleanliness before coatsoluble salts within the coating maker's limit; dust ISO 8502-3 rating ≤ 2

2. Coating systems

General structuralzinc-rich epoxy primer + epoxy intermediate + polyurethane topcoat
Wet-end / washdownhigh-build epoxy, chemically resistant, over zinc-rich primer
High-temperature (> 120 °C)inorganic silicone or silicone-aluminium, no organic topcoat
Stainless / non-ferrousuncoated unless required for identification

5. Inspection and testing

  • Ambient hold points: surface temperature ≥ 3 °C above dew point, RH ≤ 85%, recorded before each coat.
  • DFT verified per SSPC-PA 2 with a calibrated gauge; readings logged by area.
  • Adhesion by cross-cut (ASTM D3359) or pull-off (ASTM D4541) on witness coupons for the wet-end system.
  • Holiday / pinhole detection on immersion and washdown coatings per NACE SP0188 (low-voltage wet sponge for DFT < 500 µm).
  • Each stage recorded on a coating inspection report; final report issued with the Equipment.
