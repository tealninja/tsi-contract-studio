This annex defines the performance test by which the Equipment is demonstrated to meet the guaranteed parameters. The test establishes whether Seller has met its performance obligations and is the basis for the performance element of Substantial Completion.

1. Purpose

To verify, under representative and stable operating conditions, that the Equipment achieves the guaranteed throughput, product moisture, and specific energy consumption set out below, measured by agreed methods and instruments.

2. Test conditions

The test is run only after Commissioning is complete, on the reference furnish and grade, with feed within the design envelope and all utilities within their supply tolerances. Steady state is held for a stabilization period before data collection.

4. Measurement method and instruments

  • Throughput from calibrated reel/winder counters cross-checked against the stock flow and consistency measurement.
  • Moisture from the on-machine scanner, verified against oven-dry laboratory samples (TAPPI T 412) taken during the run.
  • Thermal energy from calibrated steam flow, pressure, and condensate metering; electrical energy from calibrated power metering at the drive lineups.
  • All test instruments carry current calibration certificates traceable to a national standard; calibration records are appended to the report.

5. Acceptance criteria

The Equipment passes when each guaranteed parameter is met within its stated tolerance over the measured run, after agreed corrections for off-reference ambient and utility conditions. Where results are expressed against a guarantee, performance is assessed as the corrected measured value relative to the guaranteed value.

Full acceptanceall guaranteed parameters met within tolerance
Minimum performance threshold≥ 80% of guaranteed throughput with product within moisture tolerance
Below thresholdnot accepted; Seller to remedy and re-test, subject to Article on liquidated damages / rejection

6. Repeat tests

If a test does not achieve full acceptance, Seller may make adjustments and repeat the test. Up to two (2) repeat tests are permitted. A run is disregarded and does not count against this limit where it is invalidated by a cause outside Seller's scope — including feed, furnish, or utility supply provided by Buyer that falls outside the design envelope, or an upstream or downstream process constraint that prevents the Equipment from running at its rated point (the "upstream/downstream carve-out"). Buyer shall afford reasonable production time and the reference furnish for each repeat test. [ATTY: The upstream/downstream carve-out shifts risk of Buyer-side process limits to Buyer; confirm the boundary of Seller's scope on the P&ID so the carve-out is not open-ended.]

7. Reporting

A performance test report is issued within ten (10) business days of the run, stating the measured and corrected values against each guarantee, the instruments and calibration used, the raw data, and the pass/fail determination. The report, once accepted by both parties, is the record of performance for Substantial Completion.
