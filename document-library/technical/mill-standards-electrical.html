2. Area classification

Electrical apparatus shall be selected for the area classification of its installed location. Unless stated otherwise on the classification drawing, the wet-end, hydraulic room, and dryer hood plenum are treated as ordinary (unclassified) industrial areas with high ambient moisture; enclosures in washdown zones are IP66 / NEMA 4X. Any location classified as hazardous under NFPA 70 (NEC) Article 500 shall use apparatus rated for the applicable Class, Division, and Group.

3. Cabling and conduit

Power, control, and instrument cabling shall be segregated and routed in separate raceways or with the minimum separation set by IEEE 518 to limit coupled noise. Cable sizing, derating, and overcurrent protection follow NFPA 70.

  • Power conductors: XLPE or EPR insulated copper, 600 V rated, sized for continuous load with no derated conductor above 75% of ampacity.
  • Instrument signals: individually shielded twisted pair, drain grounded at one end only (control system side).
  • VFD motor leads: symmetrical shielded VFD cable, gland-terminated with 360° shield bond at both ends.
  • Conduit: rigid galvanized steel in process areas; liquidtight flexible for final motor and instrument connections; PVC-coated in washdown zones.
  • Fill and bend radius per NFPA 70; no raceway above 40% fill.

5. Grounding and bonding

Grounding and bonding follow NFPA 70 Article 250 and IEEE 142. All enclosures, motor frames, conduit, cable trays, and exposed metalwork are bonded to the equipment grounding conductor and tied to the Site ground grid. A dedicated, isolated instrument ground reference is provided for the control system where required by the vendor, brought to a single ground point to avoid loops.

6. Labeling and identification

  • Every enclosure, device, terminal, and conductor is uniquely tagged per the project tag register and the loop diagrams.
  • Engraved laminate nameplates (white-on-black for equipment, white-on-red for hazard/arc-flash) mechanically fastened, not adhesive.
  • Arc-flash incident-energy labels applied per NFPA 70E following the Buyer's study. [INT: Arc-flash study is Buyer scope; Seller supplies equipment data to feed it.]
