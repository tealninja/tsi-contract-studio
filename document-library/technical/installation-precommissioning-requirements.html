This annex sets the site, scope, and readiness requirements for installation and pre-commissioning of the Equipment. It defines the division of work between Seller and Buyer and the conditions that must be in place before Commissioning begins. The responsibility matrix in the accompanying annex governs line-by-line allocation; this annex sets the substance.

2. Foundations and civil readiness

  • Foundations poured, cured to design strength, and surveyed within the tolerances on the foundation drawing before the Equipment is set.
  • Anchor bolts, pockets, and embedments placed to the setting plan; as-built survey issued to Seller.
  • Floor loading, drainage, and access routes for rigging confirmed clear.

4. Environmental conditions

The installation area shall be weather-tight, lit, and ventilated, with ambient held within the design range and free of construction dust before electrical and controls equipment is uncrated. Housekeeping and lay-down areas are provided by Buyer.

5. Safety and lockout/tagout

All work follows the Site safety plan. Energy isolation uses the Site lockout/tagout (LOTO) procedure; no Equipment is energized or pressurized for pre-commissioning until isolation boundaries are established and permits issued. Safety data sheets (SDS) for all commissioning fluids and lubricants are provided and reviewed before use.
