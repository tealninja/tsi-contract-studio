Buyer waives, and Seller shall not be liable for, any indirect, incidental, special, punitive, exemplary, or consequential damages, including loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of use, loss of product, cost of capital, cost of replacement power, cost of substitute equipment or services, downtime, and loss of production or opportunity, whether the claim sounds in contract, tort, strict liability, warranty, or otherwise, and even if the possibility of such damages was known. This waiver applies regardless of the failure of any exclusive or limited remedy and regardless of the basis of the claim.
  2. Aggregate liability cap. The total aggregate liability of Seller arising out of or relating to this Agreement, from all causes and across all claims combined, shall not exceed an amount equal to 15% of the Contract Price. The cap applies in the aggregate and is not per-occurrence, and it is inclusive of any amounts payable as liquidated damages, warranty costs, or indemnity.   3. Carve-outs from the cap.   4. Application of the waiver. The waiver of consequential damages in clause 1 applies to every claim under or relating to this Agreement, including the carve-outs in clause 3 and any indemnity, and is not displaced by any allegation of gross negligence or willful misconduct.

The remedies stated in this Agreement, including the warranty remedies in the Article on warranties and any liquidated damages, are Buyer's sole and exclusive remedies for the matters they address, in lieu of all other remedies at law or in equity, and Buyer waives all others.
  6. Liquidated damages within the cap. Liquidated damages payable under this Agreement count toward, and are not additional to, the aggregate cap in clause 2.
  7. Basis of the bargain. The parties acknowledge that the Contract Price reflects the allocation of risk in this Article and that these limitations are a material basis of the bargain, apply even if a limited remedy is found to have failed of its essential purpose, and would be materially different absent these limits.
