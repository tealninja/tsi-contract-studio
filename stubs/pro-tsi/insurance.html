1. Seller's insurance. Seller shall, at its own expense, carry and maintain the statutory minimum insurance required of it as an employer and operator in the jurisdiction of its place of performance, namely workers compensation, employers liability, and automobile liability at the limits required by applicable law, together with commercial general liability (CGL) at limits customary for Seller's scope. Seller's obligations under this Article are limited to the coverages stated in this clause.
  2. Workers compensation and automobile. Workers compensation at statutory limits for the jurisdiction of Seller's place of performance, employers liability at Seller's standard program limits, and business automobile liability for vehicles used by Seller in performance at the statutory minimum limit.
  3. Buyer's insurance. Buyer shall, at its own expense, carry and maintain from delivery of the first shipment through final acceptance builder's risk or property insurance on an all-risk replacement-cost basis covering the Equipment and all materials at or in transit to the Site, and shall carry CGL and any owner's or operator's coverage appropriate to the Site and to operation of the Equipment. Buyer bears the risk of loss to the Equipment at the Site and shall name Seller as an additional insured on the builder's risk and property policies. [ATTY: This is the seller-favorable opening position — Seller carries only its statutory minimums and Buyer carries builder's risk/property at Site and names Seller as additional insured. Likely fallback toward balanced is restoring Seller's stated CGL limits (2,000,000 USD per occurrence / 4,000,000 USD aggregate), umbrella/excess and E&O layers, and Buyer's additional-insured status on Seller's CGL.]
  4. Waiver of subrogation. Each party waives, and shall cause its insurers to waive, all rights of subrogation against the other party for loss covered by the policies required under this Article, to the extent of that coverage. Buyer's builder's risk and property insurers shall waive subrogation against Seller.
  5. Certificates. On written request, Seller shall deliver a certificate of insurance evidencing the coverage in clause 1. A certificate does not amend, extend, or waive the coverage the policy actually provides, and Seller is not obligated to deliver endorsement copies, renewal certificates, or notices of change.
  6. Coverage sets a minimum. The insurance in this Article sets minimum coverage and is without prejudice to the limitations on Seller's liability in the Article on limitation of liability, which control.
