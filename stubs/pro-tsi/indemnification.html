1. Seller's limited indemnity. Seller shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Buyer from and against third-party claims, damages, losses, and reasonable expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) only to the extent they arise out of Seller's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or out of the third-party Intellectual Property infringement indemnity in clause 3. Seller has no other indemnity obligation under this Agreement.
  2. Buyer's indemnity of Seller. Buyer shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless Seller, its affiliates, and their respective officers, employees, subcontractors, and agents from and against all third-party claims, damages, losses, and expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) arising out of or relating to the Site, Buyer's operation, use, or maintenance of the Equipment, the acts or omissions of Buyer or its other contractors, or Buyer's breach of this Agreement, except to the extent a loss falls within Seller's limited indemnity in clause 1. Where a loss is caused by the fault of both parties, Buyer's indemnity applies except to the proportion attributable to Seller's gross negligence or willful misconduct.
  3. Third-party IP infringement. Seller shall indemnify Buyer against claims that the Equipment as furnished directly infringes a third party's issued patent or registered copyright, and shall pay the damages finally awarded, provided Buyer meets the procedure in clause 5. If use of the Equipment is enjoined, Seller shall, at its sole option and expense, procure the right to continue use, modify the Equipment to be non-infringing, or accept return of the affected Equipment and refund the portion of the Contract Price allocable to it less reasonable depreciation. This is Buyer's sole and exclusive remedy for infringement.
  4. IP carve-outs. Seller's obligation in clause 3 does not apply to infringement arising from Buyer's designs, specifications, or instructions, from modification of the Equipment by anyone other than Seller, from combination of the Equipment with items not supplied by Seller, from use of the Equipment outside the parameters in Annex D, or from Buyer's continued use after Seller has provided a non-infringing alternative.   5. Procedure and notice. As a condition of any indemnity, the indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of the claim (and in any event within 10 days of becoming aware of it), shall not settle, compromise, or admit liability without Seller's prior written consent, shall grant Seller sole control of the defense and settlement, and shall provide reasonable cooperation at the indemnified party's expense. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying party to the extent of any resulting prejudice.
  6. Relationship to liability limits. The indemnities in this Article are subject to the limitations and exclusions in the Article on limitation of liability, including the aggregate cap and the waiver of consequential damages.
  7. Survival. This Article survives termination, expiration, or completion of the Agreement with respect to any claim arising from acts or omissions occurring before that event.
