1. Scope. This Article governs any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its formation, interpretation, breach, or termination, between Seller and Buyer.
  2. Good-faith negotiation. Before commencing arbitration, the party raising a dispute shall give written notice describing it. The parties' project representatives shall attempt to resolve the dispute in good faith within 15 days of the notice, and if they do not, senior executives with authority to settle shall confer within a further 15 days. Neither party may commence arbitration until the escalation period has expired.
  3. Binding arbitration. A dispute not resolved through negotiation and escalation shall be finally resolved by binding arbitration seated in Seattle, Washington, USA, administered under arbitration rules selected by Seller (which, absent Seller's election, shall be the Commercial Arbitration Rules of the American Arbitration Association), before a sole arbitrator, in the English language, applying the substantive law of the State of Washington. The award is final and binding and enforceable in any court of competent jurisdiction, and the parties waive any right of appeal to the extent permitted by law. [ATTY: This is the seller-favorable opening position — arbitration seated at Seller's location (Seattle, Washington), Seller's choice of rules, Washington law, and no court interim relief against Seller. Likely fallback toward balanced is a neutral seat, an institutional-rules default (e.g. ICC), a three-arbitrator panel above a threshold, and reciprocal access to court interim relief.]
  4. Interim relief. Seller may apply to a court of competent jurisdiction or to the arbitral tribunal for interim or conservatory relief, including relief to protect Confidential Information or Intellectual Property. Buyer shall seek interim or conservatory relief only from the arbitral tribunal once constituted, and shall not seek injunctive or other interim relief against Seller from any court.
  5. Continued performance. During any dispute, each party shall continue to perform its obligations under this Agreement, and Buyer shall continue to pay all amounts other than those Buyer disputes in good faith and in writing with particulars.
  6. Costs. The prevailing party in any arbitration or related proceeding shall recover its reasonable costs, including reasonable attorneys' fees and the costs of the arbitration, from the other party. Each party bears its own costs of the negotiation and escalation stages.
  7. Survival. This Article survives termination, expiration, or completion of the Agreement.
