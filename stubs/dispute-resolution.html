1. Scope. This Article governs any dispute, claim, or controversy arising out of or relating to this Agreement, including its formation, interpretation, breach, or termination, between Seller and Buyer.
  2. Good-faith negotiation. Before commencing any formal proceeding, the party raising a dispute shall give written notice describing it. The parties' project representatives shall then attempt to resolve the dispute in good faith within 15 days of the notice.
  3. Escalation. If the representatives do not resolve the dispute within that period, each party shall refer it to a senior executive with authority to settle, and those executives shall confer within a further 15 days. Neither party may commence arbitration or litigation until the escalation period has expired, except to seek the interim relief permitted in clause 6.
