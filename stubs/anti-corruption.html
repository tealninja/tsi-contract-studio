Neither party, nor anyone acting on its behalf, shall offer, promise, give, request, or accept any bribe, kickback, or other improper advantage to obtain or retain business or any improper benefit in connection with this Agreement. Each party shall comply with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA) and with any other applicable anti-corruption law, including the local law of the Site.

Neither party shall make or authorize any payment or transfer of value to a government official, political party, or candidate to influence an official act or decision, or to secure any improper advantage relating to the Equipment or its permitting, clearance, or acceptance.
