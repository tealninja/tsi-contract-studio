1. Obligation to insure. Seller shall, at its own expense, carry and maintain the insurance set out in this Article with insurers rated A− VII or better by AM Best, from the effective date of this Agreement through Substantial Completion and for any tail period stated below. The coverages and limits in Annex N control where they exceed those stated here.
  2. Commercial general liability. Commercial general liability (CGL) on an occurrence basis, covering bodily injury, property damage, products and completed operations, and contractual liability, with a limit of not less than 2,000,000 USD per occurrence and 4,000,000 USD aggregate.
  3. Workers compensation and employers liability. Workers compensation at statutory limits for the jurisdiction of the Site, and employers liability of not less than 1,000,000 USD each accident, each employee, and policy limit.
  4. Automobile liability. Business automobile liability covering owned, hired, and non-owned vehicles used in performance, with a combined single limit of not less than 1,000,000 USD per accident.
  5. Professional and umbrella. Where Seller performs design or engineering, professional liability (errors and omissions, E&O) of not less than 2,000,000 USD per claim and in the aggregate, maintained for two years after Substantial Completion. Seller shall also carry umbrella or excess liability of not less than 5,000,000 USD, sitting over the CGL, employers liability, and automobile layers. [DRAFT NOTE: For projects above 10,000,000 USD Contract Price, consider raising the umbrella layer to a limit equal to 25% of the Contract Price and confirm against Annex N.]
  6. Additional insured and primary coverage. Buyer and its affiliates shall be named additional insureds on the CGL, automobile, and umbrella policies with respect to liability arising from Seller's performance. Seller's insurance is primary and non-contributory to any insurance carried by Buyer.
  7. Waiver of subrogation. Each party waives, and shall cause its insurers to waive, all rights of subrogation against the other party for loss covered by the policies required under this Article, to the extent of that coverage. [INT: Mutual waiver assumes Buyer carries builder's risk / property on the Equipment at Site; confirm the risk-of-loss allocation in the delivery article.]
  8. Certificates and notice. Seller shall deliver certificates of insurance evidencing the required coverage before commencing work and on each renewal, and shall provide 30 days' written notice of cancellation or material reduction (10 days for non-payment of premium). A certificate does not amend, extend, or waive the coverage the policy actually provides. [ATTY: Certificates evidence but do not guarantee coverage. For high-value scopes, require endorsement copies for additional-insured and waiver-of-subrogation status rather than relying on the certificate box.]
  9. No limitation of liability. The required insurance sets minimum coverage and does not limit Seller's obligations or liability under this Agreement.
