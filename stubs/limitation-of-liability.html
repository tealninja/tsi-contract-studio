1. Waiver of consequential damages. Neither Seller nor Buyer is liable to the other for indirect, incidental, special, punitive, or consequential damages, including loss of profit, loss of revenue, loss of use, cost of capital, cost of replacement power, or loss of production, whether the claim sounds in contract, tort, strict liability, or otherwise, even if advised of the possibility of such damages. This waiver is mutual and applies regardless of the failure of any exclusive remedy.
  2. Aggregate liability cap. The total aggregate liability of Seller arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall not exceed an amount equal to the Contract Price. Where the parties agree a lower cap, it is expressed as 15% of the Contract Price. The cap applies in the aggregate across all claims and is not per-occurrence.
