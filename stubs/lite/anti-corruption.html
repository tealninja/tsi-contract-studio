1. Follow the law. Each of Seller and Buyer will comply with the laws that apply to its performance of this Agreement, including labor, health and safety, environment, and trade rules.
  2. No bribery. Neither party, nor anyone acting for it, will offer or accept a bribe, kickback, or other improper advantage in connection with this Agreement. Each party will comply with the U.S. Foreign Corrupt Practices Act (FCPA), the local anti-corruption law of the Site, and applicable sanctions and export control laws.
  3. Records. Each party will keep accurate records relating to this Agreement for at least five years, and will allow the other to audit them on reasonable notice to check compliance.
  4. Breach. A party that learns of a violation will promptly tell the other. A material breach of this clause lets the other party end the Agreement in writing, without giving up its other rights.
