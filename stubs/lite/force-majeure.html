1. What it means. Force Majeure is an event beyond a party's reasonable control that it could not avoid, such as natural disaster, war, epidemic, or an act of government. It does not include a party's inability to pay or a change in market conditions.
  2. Relief. Neither Seller nor Buyer is in breach of this Agreement to the extent Force Majeure prevents its performance, except for money already due. Affected deadlines move by the length of the delay.
  3. Notice and mitigation. The affected party will give written notice within seven days, make reasonable efforts to limit the delay, and resume performance as soon as it can. An extension of time is the only remedy; neither party owes the other for costs of the event.
  4. Long delay. If Force Majeure lasts more than 120 days in a row, either party may end the Agreement in writing. Buyer then pays for Equipment delivered and work done up to that point.
