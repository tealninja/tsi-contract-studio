1. Talk first. For any dispute under this Agreement between Seller and Buyer, the party raising it will give written notice, and the parties will try in good faith to settle it within 15 days, escalating to senior management if needed.
  2. Arbitration. A dispute not settled that way will be finally resolved by binding arbitration under the Rules of Arbitration of the International Chamber of Commerce, before a sole arbitrator, in English. The award is final and enforceable in any competent court. [DRAFT NOTE: Set the arbitration seat (e.g. Singapore for a Vietnam supply deal) before first send.]
  3. Interim relief. Either party may still ask a court for urgent interim relief, for example to protect Confidential Information, without giving up arbitration.
  4. Keep performing. During a dispute, each party keeps performing the parts not in dispute, and Buyer keeps paying undisputed amounts when due. This clause survives the end of the Agreement.
