1. Seller insures. Seller will carry, at its own cost, general liability, workers compensation, and vehicle insurance covering its work under this Agreement, at limits usual for work of this size and no less than any figure in the annex.
  2. Buyer as additional insured. Buyer is named as an additional insured on the general liability cover for claims arising from Seller's work, and Seller's insurance applies first.
  3. Proof and notice. Before starting work, Seller will give Buyer certificates showing the cover, and will give 30 days' written notice before any cancellation or material reduction.
  4. Not a liability cap. This insurance sets a minimum only and does not limit Seller's other obligations or liability under the Agreement.
