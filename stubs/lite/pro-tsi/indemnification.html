1. Seller's limited cover. Seller covers Buyer against third-party claims and reasonable costs only where they arise from Seller's gross negligence or willful misconduct, or from direct IP infringement under clause 2. Seller has no other indemnity duty under this Agreement. [ATTY: Seller-favorable opening position — narrow Seller indemnity, broad Buyer indemnity.   2. IP infringement. Seller covers Buyer if the Equipment as furnished directly infringes a third party's patent or registered copyright, and will at its option fix, replace, or refund it less depreciation. This is Buyer's sole remedy, and does not apply where the problem comes from Buyer's designs, changes, or misuse.
  3. Buyer covers Seller. Buyer covers Seller and its people against claims arising from the Site, Buyer's use of the Equipment, Buyer's contractors, or Buyer's breach, except to the extent caused by Seller's gross negligence or willful misconduct.
  4. Notice and defence. The party seeking cover must give prompt written notice, let Seller run the defence, and not settle without Seller's consent. This clause survives the end of the Agreement.
