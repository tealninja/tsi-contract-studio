1. Talk first. For any dispute under this Agreement between Seller and Buyer, the party raising it will give written notice, and the parties will try in good faith to settle it within 15 days, escalating to senior management if needed, before starting arbitration.
  2. Arbitration in Seattle. A dispute not settled that way will be finally resolved by binding arbitration seated in Seattle, Washington, USA, under rules chosen by Seller, before a sole arbitrator, in English, under Washington law. The award is final and enforceable in any competent court. [ATTY: Seller-favorable opening position — Seller's home seat, Seller's rules, prevailing-party costs, no court interim relief against Seller. Likely fallback to balanced is a neutral seat and institutional rules.]
  3. Interim relief. Seller may ask a court or the tribunal for urgent interim relief, for example to protect Confidential Information. Buyer may seek such relief only from the tribunal, not from a court.
  4. Keep performing; costs. During a dispute, each party keeps performing, and Buyer keeps paying amounts it does not dispute in good faith. The prevailing party recovers its reasonable costs and fees. This clause survives the end of the Agreement.
