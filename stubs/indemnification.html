1. Mutual indemnity. Each party (the indemnifying party) shall indemnify, defend, and hold harmless the other party, its affiliates, and their respective officers, employees, and agents from and against third-party claims, damages, losses, and reasonable expenses (including reasonable attorneys' fees) to the extent arising out of the indemnifying party's negligence, willful misconduct, or breach of this Agreement.
  2. Personal injury and property damage. Seller shall indemnify Buyer against claims for bodily injury, death, or damage to tangible property to the extent caused by the acts or omissions of Seller or its subcontractors at the Site. Buyer shall indemnify Seller on the same basis for the acts or omissions of Buyer or its other contractors. Where injury or damage is caused by the fault of both parties, each bears the indemnity in proportion to its share of fault.
  3. Third-party IP infringement. Seller shall indemnify Buyer against claims that the Equipment as furnished infringes a third party's patent, copyright, or trade secret, and shall pay the damages and costs finally awarded, provided Buyer meets the procedure in clause 5. If use of the Equipment is enjoined, Seller shall, at its option and expense, procure the right to continue use, modify the Equipment to be non-infringing while preserving its function, or refund the portion of the Contract Price allocable to the affected Equipment less reasonable depreciation.
  4. IP carve-outs. Seller's obligation in clause 3 does not apply to infringement arising from Buyer's designs or specifications, from modification of the Equipment by anyone other than Seller, from combination of the Equipment with items not supplied by Seller where the claim arises from the combination, or from Buyer's use of the Equipment outside the operating parameters in Annex D.   5. Procedure and notice. The indemnified party shall give prompt written notice of any claim, shall not settle or admit liability without the indemnifying party's prior written consent, and shall grant the indemnifying party control of the defense and reasonable cooperation at the indemnifying party's expense. Failure to give prompt notice relieves the indemnifying party only to the extent it is actually prejudiced by the delay.   6. Relationship to liability limits. The indemnities in this Article are subject to the limitations and exclusions in the Article on limitation of liability, except where that Article expressly carves out indemnity obligations.
