1. Definitions

“Agreement” means this short-form agreement and its annexes. “Seller” means Teal Sales Incorporated. “Buyer” means {{buyer}}. “Equipment” means the items in Annex A. “Site” means {{site}}. “Contract Price” means {{contractPriceFmt}}, as adjusted by agreed changes. “Commissioning” means placing the Equipment into working order. “Substantial Completion” means the Equipment is able to operate for its intended purpose.

2. Equipment, scope & delivery

  1. Supply. Seller will design, supply, and deliver the Equipment in Annex A, and provide the engineering, commissioning support, and training listed there.
  2. Delivery. Delivery is on the terms in Annex A (Incoterms 2020). Title passes on full payment for an item; risk passes on delivery.
  3. Buyer's part. Buyer will prepare the Site and provide the information, utilities, and access the Equipment needs, as noted in Annex A.
  4. Manuals. Seller will provide operation and maintenance manuals before Commissioning.

3. Price & payment

  1. Price. The Contract Price is {{contractPriceFmt}}, payable against the milestones in Annex B.
  2. Payment. Each invoice is due within 30 days. Late undisputed amounts accrue interest at 1.0% per month.
  3. Changes. A change to scope, price, or schedule is only binding if both parties sign it. Priced additions carry a {{changeOrderMarkup}}% markup.

4. Delivery delay & performance

  1. Delay LD. If Seller delivers late, after a {{delayLDGracePeriod}}-week grace period Seller pays {{delayLDRate}}% of the Contract Price per week, capped at {{delayLDCap}}%.
  2. Performance. The Equipment must meet the guarantees in Annex C. If it reaches at least {{minPerformanceThreshold}}% of guaranteed performance, performance LDs apply (capped at {{performanceLDCap}}%). These LDs are Buyer's only remedy for delay and shortfall.

5. Warranty

  1. Warranty. Seller warrants the Equipment against defects in workmanship and materials for {{warrantyPeriod}}.
  2. Remedy. Seller will repair or replace a defective item. This is Buyer's only warranty remedy. Annex C may add detail.
  3. Exclusions. The warranty does not cover misuse, normal wear, consumables, or Buyer-supplied items.
