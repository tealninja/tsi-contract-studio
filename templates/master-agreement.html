Article 1 — Definitions

The following capitalized terms have the meanings given below. Terms defined elsewhere in this Agreement have the meanings there assigned.

  1. “Agreement” means this equipment supply agreement, its cover sheet, Articles 1–22, and Annexes A through R, as amended by executed Change Orders.
  2. “Seller” means Teal Sales Incorporated.
  3. “Buyer” means {{buyer}}.
  4. “Equipment” means the machinery, components, software, and documentation Seller supplies under Annex A.
  5. “Site” means {{site}}.
  6. “Contract Price” means {{contractPriceFmt}}, as adjusted by Change Orders.
  7. “Commissioning” means the activities under Article 5 to place the Equipment into operating condition.
  8. “Substantial Completion” means the point certified under Annex K at which the Equipment is capable of operating for its intended purpose, subject to punch-list items.
  9. “Confidential Information” has the meaning in Article 18.
  10. “Intellectual Property” means patents, copyrights, trade secrets, know-how, and other proprietary rights.
  11. “Force Majeure” has the meaning in Article 16.

Article 2 — Scope of work & engineering

  1. Seller shall design, manufacture, and supply the Equipment described in Annex A, and shall perform the engineering, supervision, Commissioning, and training set out in this Agreement and its Annexes.
  2. Seller shall deliver operation and maintenance (O&M) manuals for the Equipment prior to Commissioning. [INT: O&M manuals are a delivery requirement here, not a separate annex.]
  3. Buyer shall provide the buyer-supplied information and Site conditions described in Annex C and the installation prerequisites in Annex I.
  4. Work not expressly included in Annex A is excluded unless added by executed Change Order under Article 8.

Article 3 — Delivery, title & risk of loss

  1. Delivery terms are as stated in Annex A and interpreted under Incoterms 2020. [DRAFT NOTE: Confirm Incoterm (e.g. FCA, CIP) and named place for this project.]
  2. Title to each item of Equipment passes on the later of full payment for that item or delivery, except that Intellectual Property does not pass (Article 9).
  3. Risk of loss passes in accordance with the agreed Incoterm.

Article 4 — Liquidated damages

  1. If Seller fails to achieve delivery by the date in Annex D, after a grace period of {{delayLDGracePeriod}} weeks, Seller shall pay delivery liquidated damages of {{delayLDRate}}% of the Contract Price per week of delay, capped at {{delayLDCap}}% of the Contract Price.
  2. Performance liquidated damages, if any, are governed by Article 6 and Annex F and are capped at {{performanceLDCap}}% of the Contract Price.
  3. Liquidated damages under this Agreement are in the aggregate capped at {{aggregateLDCap}}% of the Contract Price and are the Buyer's exclusive remedy for delay and performance shortfall.

Article 5 — Commissioning

  1. Seller shall issue a notice of commissioning readiness (Annex J form) when the Equipment is ready for Commissioning.
  2. Commissioning proceeds per Annex I once Buyer confirms the prerequisites are satisfied.
  3. Buyer shall make operators, feedstock, and utilities available for Commissioning as scheduled.

Article 6 — Performance testing

  1. Performance tests are conducted under the protocol in Annex F to verify the guaranteed parameters.
  2. Article 7 — Contract price & payment

  1. The Contract Price is {{contractPriceFmt}}, payable against the milestones in Annex E.
  2. Each milestone payment is due within 30 days of the corresponding milestone certificate (Annex E / milestone certificate form).
  3. Undisputed overdue amounts accrue interest at 1.0% per month or the maximum lawful rate, whichever is lower.

Article 8 — Change orders

  1. Either party may request a change. No change is binding until documented on the change-order form (Annex G) and executed by both parties.
  2. Seller-priced additive work carries a markup of {{changeOrderMarkup}}% on cost unless otherwise agreed.
  3. An executed Change Order adjusts the Contract Price and schedule accordingly and prevails per the Order of precedence.

Article 10 — Warranty

  1. Seller warrants the Equipment against defects in workmanship and materials on the terms of Seller's standard warranty (Annex H). The warranty period is {{warrantyPeriod}}.

Article 15 — Events of default & remedies

  2. On Buyer default, Seller may suspend work and pursue payment. On Seller default, Buyer may pursue the remedies in this Agreement, subject to Articles 4 and 14.

Article 22 — Miscellaneous

  1. Entire agreement. This Agreement is the entire agreement and supersedes prior understandings.
  2. Order of precedence. In conflict: (1) executed Change Orders (most recent governs), (2) cover-sheet project particulars, (3) Articles 1–22, (4) Annexes A–R (earlier letter governs). The Annex E payment schedule prevails over the cover-sheet milestone summary.
